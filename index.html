01 · Our Planet

The Only One We Know Has Life

Earth is the only planet we know of that has life. From space, what’s most noticeable is its blue color, as about 71% of its surface is covered by water.

02 · Day & Night

The Thin Line Between Light and Dark

Earth rotates once every 24 hours. At any given time, half the planet faces the Sun and the other half is in darkness. The boundary between these two regions is called the terminator, and it continuously moves across the surface.

03 · City Lights

Human Footprint Visible from Orbit

On the night side, city lights form patterns that indicate where humans live. The blinking dots on this map mark Surakarta and several other major cities. The gold dot marks Klaten, Central Java, where the creator is based.

04 · Atmosphere

The Thin Layer That Protects Us

Earth’s atmosphere is primarily composed of nitrogen (about 78%) and oxygen (about 21%). From orbit, it appears as a thin blue layer at the edge of the planet, although most of the air is actually contained within tens of kilometers below.
